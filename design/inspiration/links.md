## For the redemption line (our signature visual)

Look outside crypto: airport departure boards, queue numbers at a bank, order book depth charts, waterfall or tranche diagrams, boarding groups. Mobbin, Land-book and Are.na searches: "queue", "waitlist position", "order book", "tranche".
